## Validation actually performed

`PortfolioBuild.ValidatePhysics()` numerically measures a 30 cm drop with drag and the configured restitution, verifies spin changes outgoing bounce velocity, and verifies a ball segment crossing 0.6 m cannot skip the paddle. The output is committed in `Validation/physics-results.json` (22.308 cm rebound in the independent numerical check).

`RuntimeVerification.Run()` exercised the real generated scene in Unity play mode, including `Physics.SphereCastNonAlloc` against the actual table collider. The runtime measured **22.358 cm**, compared with the ITTF's approximately 23 cm reference. The small difference between the two checks comes from collision clearance and hit-fraction processing.

These checks validate a published bounce benchmark and specific contact invariants. They do **not** validate every aerodynamic trajectory, friction value or real controller swing.
