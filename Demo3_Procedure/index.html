PASS procedure

Quest: bring a controller to the carrying handle and HOLD the side GRIP button. A green hand marker means it is in reach. Release GRIP to place it. The index trigger selects signs/gauge or sprays while holding the body and nozzle in separate hands. Left X recenters the bench.

Desktop: drag objects (Shift = left hand). Inspection controls below use the same state checks.

Desktop: arrows sweep left / right while spraying. Click WATER to test a wrong part.
